GEN 1 DICE BATTLER

Pick two Pokemon. Dice decide everything. Stats decide the odds.

How it works (dice + real Gen 1 stats)

Each Pokemon uses its real Gen 1 base stats, converted to Level 50 battle stats (HP = Base+91, Atk/Def/Spc/Spe = Base+36). Each round both roll d20 + Speed/10 for initiative. Each attack rolls d20 + Offense/25 vs DC 12 + Defense/30 (natural 1 always misses, natural 20 always crits). On a hit, damage is d12 + d8 + Offense*0.35 − Defense*0.15 (min 8), x1.5 on a crit, x2 / x0.5 on super-effective / resisted hits. Attackers use their stronger offense (Physical=Atk vs Def, Special=Spc vs Spc). Battle ends when a Pokemon hits 0 HP and faints. Three times per battle the fight pauses for a math quiz (HP left, initiative total, hits-to-KO): each correct answer earns your (Player) Pokemon an immediate bonus attack — get all three right to really tip the battle!
